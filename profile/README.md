# HAIHQ

HAIHQ is a nonprofit, open-source research organization focused on advancing Health AI.

We research and build the models, benchmarks, and technical tools that can make artificial intelligence more useful, reliable, and accessible in healthcare. Our work connects AI research with healthcare needs and the open infrastructure required to build on that research.

HAIHQ is not a healthcare provider or a commercial software company. We contribute research, infrastructure, and open technology to the wider Health AI ecosystem.

## Mission

Advance Health AI through open research and open technology.

## Why HAIHQ exists

Healthcare has its own data structures, terminology, workflows, safety requirements, operational constraints, and evaluation needs. Progress in general-purpose AI does not address all of these challenges.

Useful Health AI needs focused research, representative benchmarks, suitable datasets, and infrastructure designed for healthcare. HAIHQ works on that foundation so researchers, engineers, and healthcare organizations can build and evaluate better systems.

## What we work on

### Models

We study and build models and model-based systems for healthcare problems. Areas of interest include language and multimodal capabilities, clinical reasoning, healthcare data understanding, and information extraction. We look at which capabilities healthcare actually requires and where specialized or smaller models may be useful.

### Benchmarks and evaluation

We develop benchmarks and evaluation methods for questions existing tests do not answer well. This includes measuring capability, reliability, robustness, safety, limitations, state awareness, tool use, and interaction with clinical data.

Our aim is to make evaluation reflect the conditions Health AI systems may encounter in practice.

### Tooling and infrastructure

We build open technical infrastructure for Health AI research and development. This can include developer tools, data and evaluation infrastructure, interoperability tools, and environments for experimentation.

A research project may produce a paper, but it may also produce a model, benchmark, dataset, library, specification, reference implementation, or other system people can inspect and use.

## How we work

- **Study healthcare-specific problems.** Healthcare data, workflows, and safety needs call for focused investigation.
- **Build things that can be tested.** We turn ideas into working systems, experiments, benchmarks, and measurable questions.
- **Measure before claiming progress.** We define capabilities and failure modes, then evaluate them with clear methods.
- **Keep work open.** Where possible, we publish code, benchmarks, specifications, datasets, experiments, and findings so others can inspect, reproduce, and extend them.
- **Treat infrastructure as research.** Models depend on the data systems, standards, and tools around them.
- **Stay connected to healthcare environments.** Our research should reflect real workflows, constraints, and data.

## Who HAIHQ is for

Our work is for researchers, engineers, clinicians, healthcare organizations, open-source contributors, students, and others advancing Health AI. They can use our models, build with our tooling, evaluate systems with our benchmarks, or adapt our infrastructure for their own work.

## Long-term direction

HAIHQ aims to build a growing body of open models, benchmarks, datasets, developer tools, research, and technical infrastructure focused on healthcare.

Our role is foundational: investigate important problems, build the tools needed to study them, improve how progress is measured, and make the resulting work available for others to build on.
